Notable

Intelligent Note-Taking Powered by AI

What is Notable?

Notable is an intelligent note-taking application that brings AI assistance directly into your writing process. It helps you take notes, generate quick summaries, and get answers to questions — all in one place.

Inspired by modern AI chatbots, generative AI assistants, large language models (LLMs), and tools like Obsidian, Notable is designed to make note-taking smarter and more efficient.

Key Features

  • AI-assisted note-taking and real-time suggestions
  • Automatic generation of quick summaries
  • Ask questions about your notes and get intelligent answers
  • Clean and focused writing experience

Who is it for?

Notable is built for students, developers, researchers, and anyone who wants AI support while capturing and organizing ideas. It is also a learning project focused on full-stack development, authentication (JWT/OAuth), caching, deployment, and the practical application of AI technologies.

Technology

Built with modern tools including FastAPI, React, SQLAlchemy, Alembic, JWT & OAuth authentication, and powered by AI models from Google Gemini and Groq. Developed using uv and Vite.
